RPE — Rate of Perceived Exertion

How hard the effort felt, 1 (easiest) to 10 (max).

  • 1–2Very easy — recovery, barely any effort
  • 3–4Easy — comfortable, could do all day
  • 5–6Moderate — working, can still talk
  • 7–8Hard — breathing heavy, talking is tough
  • 9Very hard — near your limit
  • 10Max — all-out, can't sustain
